# IELTS Academic 6.5 Study Plan

**Test:** IELTS Academic (computer in Cambodia)  
**Daily time:** 1.5–2 hours (90–120 minutes)

## Error log (copy this into a notes app)

```text
Date:
Skill: L / R / W / S
Source + URL:
Raw score (L/R):   / 40     Estimated band:
Question type missed:
Why (word trap / time / grammar / off-topic / no overview):
Correct answer / better sentence:
Word to reuse:
```

Review this log at the end of each week. Week 6 is built from it.

## Week 1: Diagnose + format

**Aim:** know the computer test, sit a real timed Listening and Reading, and read how 6 vs 6.5 is marked.

**Week 1 checkpoint:** you have a Listening /40, a Reading /40, one Task 1, one Task 2, and one full Speaking recording.

### Setup · 1.5 hours

- [ ] Read the Academic format: [ielts.org Academic test](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test) and [Cambridge format](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/ielts/test-format/)
- [ ] Bookmark [Academic sample tests](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test)
- [ ] Download these three PDFs (one file each, answers inside) so you can skip the messy British Council menus: [Reading](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf), [Writing](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf), [Listening](https://s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/ielts-web-static/production/Sample-tests/ielts-listening-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] Read band **6 and 6.5 only** in [Writing descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf) and [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf)
- [ ] Create free accounts: [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/free?lang=en-EN), [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/), [IELTS Online Tests](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/), optional [IELTS Ready](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare/ielts-ready)
- [ ] Done when: descriptors are saved on your phone

### Diagnostic Listening · 1.7 hours

- [ ] **Timed 30 min.** Full Listening: [IELTS Mock Test 2026 January Listening Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2026-january-listening-practice-test-1). One play only. Type answers while the audio plays. Skip any extra transfer time
- [ ] Score /40. Copy the site’s /40 and band into the Diagnostic row of the score tracker and the error log
- [ ] 30 min review: listen again only to missed items; read the transcript
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 1: [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/): record 8–10 answers about home / work / study
- [ ] Done when: raw score is written down (target later: 26+)

### Diagnostic Academic Reading · 1.8 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Full Academic Reading: [IELTS Mock Test 2025 January Reading Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-january-reading-practice-test-1-0) (3 texts, 40 questions). This is the first Reading paper in the [2026 January collection](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/collection/ielts-mock-test-2026-january)
- [ ] Score /40. Copy the site’s /40 and band into the Diagnostic row of the score tracker. Log every wrong question type (TFNG, headings, summary…)
- [ ] 20 min: read [Liz True/False/Not Given](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-true-false-not-given-essential-tips/) against *your* TFNG mistakes
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 2 from [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/): 1 min notes, 2 min talk, record
- [ ] Done when: raw score is written (target later: 27+)

### Writing sample + first Task 1 · 2 hours

- [ ] 25 min: skim [Academic Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf): look at **band 6 vs 7** examiner comments, not only band 9
- [ ] 20 min: [Liz Academic Task 1](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-1-lessons-and-tips/): how to write an **overview**
- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** Write Task 1: [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) → **Academic Writing Sample task Part 1**
- [ ] Paste it into [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/free?lang=en-EN). Then mark Task Achievement yourself using the official descriptors (did you write an overview? did you pick key numbers?)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking: [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/) skill practice
- [ ] Done when: 150+ words, overview sentence exists, word count noted

### Task 2 + full Speaking · 2 hours

- [ ] 25 min: [Liz Task 2](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2/): essay structure, when to give an opinion
- [ ] **Timed 40 min.** Task 2: [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) → **Academic Writing Sample task Part 2**
- [ ] 15 min: compare with a model on [Simon Task 2](https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/ielts-writing-task-2/): copy **structure**, not sentences
- [ ] **Timed 14 min.** Full Speaking Test 1: [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/) Parts 1–3, record
- [ ] Listen back with [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf): circle Fluency issues only today
- [ ] Done when: 250+ words and a 2-minute Part 2 recording exist

### Question-type map + grammar · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 40 min: open [ielts.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) and complete **two** Listening question types you got wrong on Diagnostic Listening (map/notes/MCQ). Use headphones
- [ ] 25 min: [B1–B2 grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/grammar/b1-b2-grammar): articles *or* present perfect *or* complex sentences (pick your weakest from the essay)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 3 questions from BC Test 1
- [ ] 10 min error log + 8 words
- [ ] Done when: those two Listening types are redone with the answer key

### Reading types + book check · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 20 min: [Liz matching headings](https://ieltsliz.com/matching-headings/)
- [ ] 40 min untimed: [Academic Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf): Matching Headings **and** Matching Features only. Check answers in the same PDF
- [ ] 20 min: rewrite the introduction and one body paragraph from Task 2 + full Speaking (clearer topic sentence)
- [ ] 15 min: [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english): latest episode, notes on 5 phrases
- [ ] Done when: headings exercise is scored

## Week 2: Question-type technique

**Aim:** stop losing marks to method. TFNG, headings, maps, Task 1 overview, 4-paragraph Task 2.

**Week 2 checkpoint:** you can explain TFNG, headings, and Task 1 overview in one sentence each.

### TFNG · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 20 min: reread [Liz TFNG](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-true-false-not-given-essential-tips/)
- [ ] 40 min: TFNG + Note Completion in the [Academic Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] Also try the official interactive TFNG on [ielts.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) (Marie Curie Identifying Information)
- [ ] 20 min Writing: plan only (no full essay) for a Task 2 opinion question from [Liz Task 2](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2/): 4-box plan: intro / body 1 / body 2 / conclusion
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 1 record
- [ ] Done when: every TFNG miss is labelled TRUE-trap, FALSE-trap, or NG-trap

### Listening maps + notes · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 50 min: map/diagram + note completion from [ielts.org Listening samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) and/or [Listening sample PDF](https://s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/ielts-web-static/production/Sample-tests/ielts-listening-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] Extra computer-style items: leftover **Listening** sample tasks on [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) (map, notes, MCQ): one type only
- [ ] 25 min: [Liz Listening](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-listening/): spelling, plurals, word-limit traps
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 2 (new cue card from [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/))
- [ ] 10 min error log
- [ ] Done when: you can explain why a wrong map answer was left/right confusion or a missed keyword

### Task 1 language · 1.8 hours

- [ ] 20 min: [Liz Task 1](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-1-lessons-and-tips/): comparisons, trends, process vs chart
- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** New Task 1 from the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) (pick a chart you have not written yet)
- [ ] Submit to [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/free?lang=en-EN)
- [ ] 25 min Reading: summary completion in the [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 3: extend every answer to 3–4 sentences
- [ ] Done when: Task 1 has an overview that does **not** list every number

### Task 2 timed + model · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 40 min.** Task 2 (discussion *or* advantages/disadvantages) from [Liz practice questions](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2/)
- [ ] 20 min: [Linking words](https://ieltsliz.com/linking-words-for-writing/): replace 3 weak linkers in your essay (*and*, *so*, *but*)
- [ ] 20 min: read one [Simon Task 2](https://www.ielts-simon.com/ielts-help-and-english-pr/ielts-writing-task-2/) model. Underline topic sentences only
- [ ] **Timed 14 min.** Full Speaking Test 2: a new cue card from [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/)
- [ ] Done when: essay is 250–290 words (over 320 usually means poor control)

### Reading Section 2 + review · 1.8 hours

- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** One passage from the [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf): Matching Headings (p. 15) **or** Matching Features (p. 3)
- [ ] 30 min review of all Week 2 errors
- [ ] 20 min grammar: complex sentences on [Perfect English Grammar](https://www.perfect-english-grammar.com/) (relative clauses *or* conditionals)
- [ ] 15 min [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/) full short test if unlocked
- [ ] 15 min [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english)
- [ ] Done when: the error-log review is written (3 repeating problems)

### Matching + sentence endings · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 50 min: Matching Sentence Endings + Sentence Completion in [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) and interactive tasks on [ielts.org](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test)
- [ ] 25 min Writing: Task 1 process *or* map: if no official process today, use Liz Task 1 process lesson on [the Task 1 page](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-1-lessons-and-tips/) then write 150 words
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 1 + paraphrase practice (do not repeat the question word-for-word)
- [ ] Done when: matching exercise is scored

### Listening Parts 3–4 · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 40 min: table completion + sentence completion (lecture / discussion) from [ielts.org samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) and [Listening PDF](https://s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/ielts-web-static/production/Sample-tests/ielts-listening-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 20 min: rewrite the Task 2 conclusion from Task 2 timed + model so it matches your position
- [ ] 15 min Part 2 recording
- [ ] Done when: Parts 3–4 question types are in the error log

## Week 3: Timed sections

**Aim:** move from untimed technique to the real clock. Writing twice this week under full 60 minutes.

**Week 3 checkpoint:** second Listening score, second Reading score, one full 60-min Writing. Reading should be moving toward 24+ if you started near 5.5.

### Timed Listening · 1.7 hours

- [ ] **Timed 30 min.** Full Listening: [IELTS Mock Test 2026 January Listening Practise Test 2](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2026-january-listening-practise-test-2). One play. Type as you listen. Skip any extra transfer time
- [ ] 25 min review + transcript
- [ ] 20 min: type a Task 1 overview + 2 body paragraphs (no intro) from a unused chart in the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 3
- [ ] Done when: Listening /40 and band are in the Week 3 score tracker row (compare with Diagnostic)

### Timed Reading passage · 1.7 hours

- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** One unused passage in the [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) (hardest remaining type). Stop at 20:00 even if unfinished
- [ ] 25 min: [Liz Reading](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-reading-lessons-information-and-tips/): time strategy (20 min per passage)
- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** Task 1 full
- [ ] Submit Task 1 to [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/free?lang=en-EN)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 2
- [ ] Done when: you stopped at 20:00 even if unfinished (then finish for review)

### Full Writing 60 min · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Task 1 (20) + Task 2 (40). Type both: [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) → **Academic Writing test** (computer) if you have not used it yet; otherwise unused prompts in the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 20 min: self-mark with [Writing descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf): four scores, then average with Task 2 counting more
- [ ] 15 min: [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/)
- [ ] 10 min: 8 academic words from Task 2 topic
- [ ] Done when: both tasks are typed, timed, and self-banded

### Timed Academic Reading Test 2 · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Full Academic Reading: [IELTS Mock Test 2026 January Reading Practise Test 2](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2026-january-reading-practise-test-2) (3 texts, 40 questions)
- [ ] 30 min: copy the /40 and band into the Week 3 score tracker row, then error log by question type
- [ ] 15 min [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english) **or** a 6–8 min [TED Talk](https://www.ted.com/talks) with English subtitles off on second play
- [ ] 15 min full Speaking (record)
- [ ] Done when: second Reading /40 and band are in the Week 3 score tracker row

### Examiner comments + speaking · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 40 min: read **all** examiner comments in [Academic Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf). Write 5 phrases the examiner praised at band 6–7 (not band 9)
- [ ] 20 min: fix the Task 2 from Full Writing 60 min: grammar only (one pass)
- [ ] **Timed 14 min.** New full Speaking using [Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/) cue cards if BC tests are used up
- [ ] 15 min grammar drill from your essay errors: [LearnEnglish grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/grammar/b1-b2-grammar)
- [ ] Done when: 5 examiner phrases are in your notes

### Listening computer format · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 50 min: leftover **Listening** sample tasks on [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test) (click, drag, gap-fill as on test day)
- [ ] 25 min Reading: diagram / flow-chart from [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min Part 1 speaking (work, study, hometown: high frequency)
- [ ] Done when: you have used highlighting/notes once on the [IELTS.org Academic Reading test](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test)

### Task 2 + reading samples · 1.7 hours

- [ ] **Timed 40 min.** Task 2 from [Write & Improve IELTS Test Zone](https://writeandimprove.com/testzone-ielts) if available, else [Liz Task 2](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2/)
- [ ] 25 min: table completion and summary completion in the [Academic Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf). Check the answers in that PDF
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 3 on the same Task 2 topic (education, environment, technology, health)
- [ ] 10 min error log
- [ ] Done when: Task 2 is timed and submitted for feedback

## Week 4: Accuracy under pressure

**Aim:** finish remaining official samples. Start Cambridge book tests if you have them (Academic 18–20). Type everything.

**Week 4 checkpoint:** official sample PDFs largely done. You are typing under time. Listening/Reading raw scores should be trending up, even if slowly.

### Official Listening leftovers · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 55 min: remaining tasks in [Listening sample PDF](https://s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/ielts-web-static/production/Sample-tests/ielts-listening-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) and any unused [ielts.org Listening samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test)
- [ ] 20 min: short Task 1 (process) 150 words
- [ ] 15 min Speaking Part 2
- [ ] 10 min spelling list (Listening wrong spellings)
- [ ] Done when: official Listening samples are finished or listed as “saved for Week 5”

### Official Reading leftovers · 1.8 hours

- [ ] 60 min: remaining tasks in [Academic Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf): especially Yes/No/Not Given, multiple choice more than one answer, diagram label
- [ ] 20 min: [Liz Reading](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-reading-lessons-information-and-tips/) on your worst remaining type
- [ ] 15 min [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/)
- [ ] Done when: sample PDF types you had not practised are ticked

### Full Writing 60 min · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Type both tasks: unused prompts in the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) or [Write & Improve IELTS Test Zone](https://writeandimprove.com/testzone-ielts)
- [ ] 20 min: compare your Task 2 with examiner-marked scripts in that PDF
- [ ] 15 min Speaking full test (record)
- [ ] Done when: self-band is written for TA/CC/LR/GRA

### Cambridge or BC computer Reading · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Cambridge IELTS Academic 18 Test 1 Reading if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 December Reading Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-december-reading-practise-test-1)
- [ ] 30 min review
- [ ] 15 min [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english)
- [ ] 15 min Part 2 + Part 3
- [ ] Done when: Reading /40 and band are in the Week 4 score tracker row

### Cambridge or official Listening + rest mix · 1.8 hours

- [ ] **Timed 30 min.** Cambridge IELTS Academic 18 Test 1 Listening if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 December Listening Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-december-listening-practise-test-1). One play. Skip any extra transfer time
- [ ] 25 min review
- [ ] 20 min: [LearnEnglish listening](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening) B2 lecture-style
- [ ] 15 min Speaking: slow down; mark pronunciation against descriptors
- [ ] Light: walk / sleep. Do not add a third mock
- [ ] Done when: Listening /40 and band are in the Week 4 score tracker row, next to Diagnostic and Week 3

### Task 1 accuracy · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 20 min: collect a **personal Task 1 phrase bank** (overview, increase, contrast, process sequence). Keep it to 25 lines so you actually memorise it
- [ ] **Timed 20 min.** New chart Task 1
- [ ] 25 min Reading skimming drill: 8 minutes preview + questions on one passage in the [Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min Part 1
- [ ] Done when: phrase bank exists in your notes

### Task 2 ideas + grammar · 1.7 hours

- [ ] 15 min: pick 4 common topics (education, environment, technology, health). Write 3 ideas each: no essay yet
- [ ] **Timed 40 min.** Task 2 on one of those topics
- [ ] [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/free?lang=en-EN)
- [ ] 20 min: grammar from [B1–B2](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/grammar/b1-b2-grammar) matching your GRA errors
- [ ] 15 min Part 3 on the same topic
- [ ] Done when: idea bank for 4 topics exists

## Week 5: Full mocks

**Aim:** two nearly full tests (Listening + Reading + Writing in one sitting). Speaking recorded after. Exam stamina.

Sit at a desk. Headphones. No phone except the timer.

**Week 5 checkpoint:** two mock overall estimates. If Writing is still 5.5–6.0, next week is Writing-heavy. If Reading is stuck below 24/40, next week is Reading-heavy.

### Mock A, papers 1–2 · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed ~100 min.** Listening then Academic Reading, **no break longer than 5 min**. Cambridge IELTS Academic 18 Test 2 if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 November Listening Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-november-listening-practise-test-1), then [IELTS Mock Test 2025 November Reading Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-november-reading-practise-test-1)
- [ ] 15 min break
- [ ] 10 min: score later tonight or tomorrow morning: do not peek during the test
- [ ] Done when: both papers finished in one sitting

### Mock A Writing + review L/R · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Full Writing (type)
- [ ] 30 min: score the Listening and Reading from Mock A, papers 1–2. Copy /40 and bands into the Mock A score tracker row and the error log
- [ ] 15 min: record Speaking Parts 1–3 ([Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/))
- [ ] Self-band Writing with [descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf)
- [ ] Done when: Mock A four skills have numbers or estimated bands

### Weak-type surgery from Mock A · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 50 min: only the question types you missed on Mock A (e.g. headings + Part 4 lecture)
- [ ] Use [Liz](https://ieltsliz.com/) lesson for that type, then 10 official items
- [ ] 20 min: rewrite the worst Task 2 body paragraph
- [ ] 15 min Speak & Improve
- [ ] Done when: that question type is practised, not just “reviewed in your head”

### Mock B, papers 1–2 · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed ~100 min.** Listening then Academic Reading, one sitting. Cambridge IELTS Academic 18 Test 3 or 19 Test 1 if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 October Listening Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-october-listening-practise-test-1), then [IELTS Mock Test 2025 October Reading Practise Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-october-reading-practise-test-1)
- [ ] One play of the Listening audio. Skip any extra transfer time
- [ ] Done when: finished without extra plays of the audio

### Mock B Writing + Speaking · 2 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Writing
- [ ] 20 min: copy Mock B Listening and Reading /40 and bands into the Mock B score tracker row and the error log
- [ ] **Timed 14 min.** Full Speaking, record, then listen with [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min: sleep/schedule check for the real test (ID, computer rules)
- [ ] Done when: Mock B estimated overall band is written

### Writing quality · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 20 min: Task 1 overview drill: 3 charts, overview only (5 min each) from remaining official/book visuals
- [ ] **Timed 40 min.** Task 2 only
- [ ] 15 min: linking and reference (*this*, *these*, *such*) using [Liz linking words](https://ieltsliz.com/linking-words-for-writing/)
- [ ] Done when: three overviews are written without copying axes titles word for word

### Speaking stamina + light L · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 40 min: two Part 2 talks + one Part 3 set. Record. Second Part 2 must hit 1:45–2:00
- [ ] 30 min: Listening Part 4 only (the lecture) from [IELTS Mock Test 2025 August Listening Practice Test 2](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-august-listening-practice-test-2). One play of that part. Leave the rest of that test unused
- [ ] 15 min [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english)
- [ ] Done when: two Part 2 recordings are near 2 minutes

## Week 6: Weak-skill surgery + peak mock

**Aim:** extra time on the lowest skill from the error log. One computer familiarisation. One peak mock, then start tapering volume.

**Default split if scores are similar:** 40% Writing, 25% Speaking, 20% Reading, 15% Listening.

**Week 6 checkpoint:** peak mock done. You are no longer learning new question types. You are repeating your personal mistakes until they shrink.

### Computer familiarisation · 1.7 hours

- [ ] Full pass of computer samples on [IELTS.org Academic samples](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test): click **Listening test**, **Academic Reading test**, **Academic Writing test** if any remain unused
- [ ] Type 20 min Task 1 from an unused chart in the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf) if you still have energy
- [ ] Watch one [Take IELTS Official](https://www.youtube.com/@TakeIELTSOfficial) computer-test video
- [ ] Done when: you know how to highlight, navigate questions, and submit on screen

### Lowest skill · 1.8 hours

- [ ] If **Writing** lowest: 60 min timed both tasks + descriptor mark + [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com/testzone-ielts)
- [ ] If **Reading** lowest: 60 min timed full Academic Reading. Cambridge leftover if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 August Reading Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-august-reading-practice-test-1)
- [ ] If **Listening** lowest: full timed Listening, then the transcript for missed items. Cambridge leftover if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 August Listening Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-august-listening-practice-test-1). One play. Skip any extra transfer time
- [ ] If **Speaking** lowest: two full interviews + [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com/) + descriptors
- [ ] 15 min: the opposite skill, short
- [ ] Done when: today’s work matches the actual lowest mock skill

### Second-lowest skill · 1.7 hours

- [ ] Same rules as Lowest skill for the next-weakest paper. For Listening or Reading, use 2025 August Test 1 of that skill if it is still unused: [Listening](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-august-listening-practice-test-1) or [Reading](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-august-reading-practice-test-1). A Cambridge leftover comes first when you own the book
- [ ] 20 min: unused table or summary items in the [Academic Reading sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-reading-sample-tasks-2023.pdf)
- [ ] 15 min Speaking even if Speaking is not the target (keep the mouth warm)
- [ ] Done when: both weak papers had a serious session this week

### Peak mock (Listening + Reading + Writing) · 3 hours

This is the last heavy three-paper day.

- [ ] Cambridge IELTS Academic 19 or 20 Test 1 if you have the book. Otherwise [IELTS Mock Test 2025 September Listening Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-september-listening-practice-test-1), then [IELTS Mock Test 2025 September Reading Practice Test 1](https://ieltsonlinetests.com/ielts-mock-test-2025-september-reading-practice-test-1). Writing prompts: unused tasks in the [Writing sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf). Skip any paid writing score on IELTS Online Tests
- [ ] Listening → 5 min stretch → Reading → 10 min snack → Writing 60 min
- [ ] Do **not** score until Score peak mock
- [ ] Done when: three papers completed under test rules

### Score peak mock + Speaking · 1.7 hours

- [ ] Score L/R. Copy the /40 and bands into the Peak score tracker row. Estimate W with descriptors. Record full Speaking
- [ ] Write **final three problems** (example: “no overview”, “NG vs F”, “Part 2 under 1:20”)
- [ ] 20 min: only those three problems
- [ ] Confirm test centre, ID, and start time from your booking email
- [ ] Done when: peak-mock band estimate is on paper

### Fix the three problems · 1.5 hours

- [ ] 30 min problem 1  
- [ ] 30 min problem 2  
- [ ] 20 min problem 3  
- [ ] 10 min easy Speaking Part 1
- [ ] No new full mock
- [ ] Done when: each problem had a drill, not a reread

### Light full Writing + logistics · 1.5 hours

- [ ] **Timed 60 min.** Last full Writing. Stop. Do not rewrite tonight
- [ ] 15 min: pack ID plan, headphones policy (centre provides headset on computer tests: check your confirmation email)
- [ ] 15 min: [IELTS Official YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@IELTSofficial) test-day video
- [ ] Done when: last Writing is filed; test-day bag list exists

## Final stretch: Taper

**Aim:** stay sharp, sleep, no new heavy mocks in the 24 hours before the test. If your exam is later in the test window, repeat the last two days until the day before the test.

### Light skills · 1.2 hours

- [ ] 20 min: reread **your** Task 1 phrase bank and 4-topic idea bank
- [ ] 20 min: 10 TFNG items max (old errors only)
- [ ] 15 min: Part 1 + one short Part 2
- [ ] 10 min: [BBC 6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english)
- [ ] Done when: you stopped at 70 minutes

### Speaking + sleep · 1 hour

- [ ] 25 min: one full Speaking, record, listen once
- [ ] 20 min: linking words + 10 verbs for charts
- [ ] 15 min: skim [Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) band 6.5
- [ ] Early night
- [ ] Done when: no extra mock was started

### If test is tomorrow, stop at 45 min · 0.8 hours

- [ ] 15 min Part 1
- [ ] 15 min read one Task 2 plan (not a full essay)
- [ ] 15 min check booking email, ID, route, water
- [ ] No new Cambridge test
- [ ] Done when: logistics are confirmed

### Test window opens · 0.5 hours

If you sit today:

- [ ] Eat. Arrive early. Computer: check you are in the right skill on screen before the recording starts
- [ ] Listening: answer as you hear; guess if needed; no blank boxes
- [ ] Reading: 20 min per passage; leave nothing blank
- [ ] Writing: Task 2 first if you consistently run out of time on Task 2; otherwise 20/40 as trained
- [ ] Speaking: 2-minute Part 2; ask the examiner to repeat a question rather than freeze

If you sit next week:

- [ ] Repeat the Speaking + sleep day
- [ ] One optional 30-min Listening section only, then stop
